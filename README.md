## The line

> My phone dies at 4 p.m. daily, but lives just long enough at 3:59 to ask if I want to back up photos I'll never look at.

## Why this line

One gadget — the phone — in one daily ritual: it dies at 4 p.m., yet survives to 3:59 for one last, useless backup prompt about photos that will never be looked at. The concrete times (4 p.m., 3:59) ground the roast, and the twist is that its final act of life is spent on the least useful task possible.

Run `main.py`; it writes the entry line plus a trailing newline to `/output/entry.txt`.
